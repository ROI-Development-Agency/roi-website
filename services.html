01 — Foundation

Brand Guidelines

One name, one look, one voice, everywhere.

Most wellness brands feel one way in the room and another way online. The space is calm and considered; the Instagram grid is a jumble of fonts, filters, and half-finished ideas. Brand guidelines close that gap — so the first impression someone gets on their phone matches the experience you've already built.

We start with what makes your studio, practice, or coaching different, then turn it into a working system: how the logo is used, the exact colors and fonts, the kind of photography that fits, and the voice that sounds like you — warm, clear, and confident. Not a 90-page book that gathers dust, but templates and rules your team can actually use on a Tuesday morning.

You keep everything. The Canva templates for posts, stories, event flyers, and your class schedule. The refreshed Instagram bio and highlight covers. And the action plan from your free Brand & Visibility Audit, so you know exactly what to fix first.

Typically Includes
  • Visual identity — logo, colors, fonts, photo style
  • Tone of voice
  • One listing profile
  • Canva templates — posts, stories, flyers, schedule
  • Instagram refresh — bio & highlight covers
  • The audit action plan
02 — Discovery

Local SEO & Visibility

Get found when someone searches for a studio near them.

When someone in your city searches "pilates near me" or "bridal fitness coach," you either show up — or your competitor does. Local SEO is the patient, compounding work of making sure it's you: the right profile, the right listings, the right words on your site, and a steady stream of real reviews.

We start with your Google Business Profile, because it's where most local searches are won or lost. Then we clean up your listings across Google, Yelp, and the directories that matter, tune your website copy for the terms your clients actually type, and build a simple system for asking happy members for reviews — and replying to them well.

SEO without conversion is a waste. We optimize the whole path: getting the right people to your page, then giving them the clearest, fastest way to book.

Typically Includes
  • Google Business Profile optimization
  • Listing cleanup across directories
  • Keyword-optimized website copy
  • Review requests & reply drafts
  • Local directory backlinks
  • Monthly ranking reports
03 — Channel

Social Media

A feed that looks as good as your space feels.

For a wellness brand, social media is the window people look through before they ever walk in. Most studios and coaches treat it like a chore — post when there's time, hope for likes, fall quiet for two weeks. That inconsistency quietly costs you bookings.

We run social as a growth system. Every post has a job: show the experience, build trust, nudge someone to book a class or a consult. We build a content engine around Instagram and Facebook, make Reels from your own footage, promote your events and classes, and keep it all consistent — so you never go dark again.

That means a content calendar, creative and captions, scheduling and posting, community replies, and monthly reporting that tells you what's actually driving bookings — not vanity likes.

Typically Includes
  • Content strategy & calendar
  • Creative & captions
  • Reels from your own footage
  • Event & class promos
  • Scheduling, posting & replies
  • Monthly performance reports
04 — Owned

Email Marketing

The highest-return channel — finally done properly.

Email is the only channel you actually own. No algorithm can bury you. When a member or prospective client gives you their address, that's a direct line — and for studios and coaches it converts at rates social media can only dream about.

Most wellness brands waste it: a dusty list, the occasional generic blast, no plan. We treat email as the precision tool it is — welcome sequences for new leads, class and event reminders that fill the room, win-back notes for members who've drifted, and campaigns that drive revenue when you need it.

We build the full system on your platform of choice (Mailchimp, Klaviyo, MailerLite, Brevo, ConvertKit) and report on what every email earns.

Typically Includes
  • Email strategy & roadmap
  • Welcome & nurture flows
  • Class & event reminders
  • Campaign design & copy
  • List growth strategy
  • Revenue & open-rate reporting
05 — Paid

Paid Advertising

We don't just run ads. We engineer them to book.

There's a reason most wellness ad accounts underperform: no one invested in the work before launch. The creative is forgettable, the targeting is lazy, and the landing page leaks the bookings the ad paid for.

We build paid campaigns the way performance engineers should. Tight local audiences — the right neighborhoods, the right intent. Creative that shows the real experience. Landing pages built to turn a click into a booked intro or a filled class. And reporting that ties spend to actual outcomes, not just clicks.

We run paid across Meta (Facebook & Instagram) and Google, starting with what makes sense for your audience and your offer — not what looks impressive in a pitch deck.

Typically Includes
  • Campaign strategy & structure
  • Ad creative & copywriting
  • Local audience targeting & testing
  • Landing page optimization
  • Budget management
  • ROAS reporting & optimization

Wellness Website Design

A site built around how people find and book you.

Your website is where every other marketing dollar eventually lands. Social sends people there. Ads send people there. Local search sends people there. If the site is slow or confusing, you're paying to fill a bucket with holes.

We design and build wellness sites that earn the traffic. Fast loading. Beautiful, calm typography. A clear class schedule and intro offer. And — critically — a booking path built around how your clients actually decide, not the page templates of whoever built the last one.

Whether you need a fresh build, a redesign, or a high-converting landing page for a campaign, we deliver — across WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, or custom builds.
